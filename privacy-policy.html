ProtoFox Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

ProtoFox is a desktop app for designing device mockups and clickable prototypes, made by Abinash Mohanty. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing. ProtoFox does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Your projects

Your designs are saved only on your own computer: in the files you save, and in an autosave copy kept in the app's local storage on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere by ProtoFox.

Internet access

ProtoFox works offline. It only uses the internet when you choose to: for example, when a prototype you build opens a web link. Websites you export from ProtoFox load fonts from Google Fonts when they are viewed in a browser; that happens on the site you publish, not in the app.

Children

ProtoFox does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
